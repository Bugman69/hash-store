History

Over this range

Average TH/s counts time offline as zero, so it shows what the miner actually delivered. History is kept on your Umbrel for 30 days, one sample per miner per minute. Removing a miner deletes its history.

Pool & payout settings

Fallback pools (optional)

Used by a miner only if your own stratum server stops answering. Each fallback must be a pool for the same coin as its row. Leave the username empty to reuse your payout address for that coin.

Saving does not touch running miners. They pick up new pool and fallback settings the next time you switch their coin, or when you push.

Double-check every address character by character. A block paid to a mistyped address is gone.

Alerts

HashHub tells you when a miner goes offline, hashes at 0 TH/s, drops to its fallback pool, or a node becomes unreachable, and again when it recovers. Everything also appears in Activity below.
